## Direct Answer (first 100 words)
Agentic AI for a US SMB starts at $10,000–20,000 for a POC, $30,000–75,000 for a production MVP, and $75,000–220,000 for a full multi-agent system. Add $250–3,500/month in LLM API costs (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source) plus $200–1,000/month for infrastructure and observability. ROI typically arrives in 9–14 months when the agent replaces at least 2 FTEs or eliminates a recurring operational bottleneck.
