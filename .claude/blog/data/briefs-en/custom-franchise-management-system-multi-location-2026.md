## Direct Answer
A custom franchise management system for multi-location US operations costs $40,000–180,000 in 2026. Covers royalty tracking (percentage-of-gross or flat fee), FDD compliance reporting, multi-unit dashboards consolidating 10–500 locations, POS integration, state-by-state franchise registration tracking (registration states), and FTC Franchise Rule audit trails. Off-the-shelf (FranConnect, Naranga, FranchiseSoft) costs $200–2,000/unit/year — custom wins above 50 units or with non-standard royalty structures.
