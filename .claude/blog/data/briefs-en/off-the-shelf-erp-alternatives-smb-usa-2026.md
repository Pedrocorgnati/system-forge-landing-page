## Direct Answer (first 100 words)
US SMBs in 2026 have five practical off-the-shelf ERP options: QuickBooks Online ($30-235/month), Xero ($15-78/month), Zoho One ($45/user/month), Odoo Enterprise ($31.10/user/month + apps), Acumatica (custom quote, ~$1,800-3,500/month entry) and NetSuite ($999/month + $99/user). Each wins in a specific zone. QuickBooks fits sub-$5M services; NetSuite fits multi-entity $20M+; Odoo and Acumatica fit manufacturing or distribution that QuickBooks can't model. A custom ERP becomes rational when off-the-shelf forces 6+ paid integrations plus manual reconciliation that costs 1+ FTE.
