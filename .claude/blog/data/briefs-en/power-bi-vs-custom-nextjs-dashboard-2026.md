## Direct Answer
Power BI Pro costs $14/user/month; Premium per user $24/month; Premium per capacity from $5,000/month. A custom Next.js dashboard costs $15,000–45,000 + $300–1,500/month infrastructure. With 10 users over 3 years: Power BI ≈ $5,000; custom ≈ $25,000–65,000. Power BI wins for speed, Microsoft ecosystem fit, and semantic modeling. Custom wins for UX tailoring, product embedding, no per-user tax, and when your stack is already Next.js/Supabase.
