## Direct Answer (first 100 words)
A production bug at Friday night in 2026 has three realistic resolution paths: (1) freelance on-call developer through trusted referrals or vetted networks (Toptal, Codementor SOS) at $150-450/hour with a 1-3 hour median response; (2) your existing vendor's emergency line (if you have a retainer) at typically 2x normal rate and a contractual SLA; (3) escalation inside your own team, which most SMBs do not have staffed on weekends. The best move in the first 10 minutes: capture exact error, last successful state, what changed, and screenshot the failing path before contacting anyone.
