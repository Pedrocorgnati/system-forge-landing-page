## Persona

## Direct Answer (first 100 words)
Choose QuickBooks Online when your business has standard accounting, sub-$5M revenue, single legal entity, no manufacturing, no multi-warehouse with batch tracking, and no commission structure beyond flat percentages. Choose a custom ERP when QuickBooks forces you to maintain 3+ side spreadsheets, when you pay 4-6 paid integrations to fill gaps (commissions, route planning, lot tracking, manufacturing), or when manual reconciliation eats more than $30k/year of staff time. QuickBooks Online: $30-235/month. Custom ERP for SMB: $25,000-90,000 initial build plus $300-1,500/month infrastructure.
