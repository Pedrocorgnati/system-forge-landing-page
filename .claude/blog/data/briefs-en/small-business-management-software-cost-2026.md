## Direct Answer
Off-the-shelf small business management software in the US costs $25–150/user/month (QuickBooks, Zoho One, NetSuite SuiteStart, Odoo). A custom system starts at $20,000 and can exceed $60,000 + $500–2,000/month maintenance. For up to 10 users, off-the-shelf wins the first 2–3 years. For 15+ users or highly custom processes, custom pays back in 18–30 months.
