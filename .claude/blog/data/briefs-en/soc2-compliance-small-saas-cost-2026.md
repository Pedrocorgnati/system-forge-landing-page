## Direct Answer
SOC 2 Type I costs $12,000–30,000 and takes 3–6 months (point-in-time assessment). Type II costs $25,000–80,000, takes 6–12 months, and requires a 6-month observation period first. Add $4,000–25,000/year for compliance platform (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe), plus employee time for evidence collection (1 FTE ~3 months concentrated effort). Many startups start Type I to unlock enterprise deals, then move to Type II within 12 months.
