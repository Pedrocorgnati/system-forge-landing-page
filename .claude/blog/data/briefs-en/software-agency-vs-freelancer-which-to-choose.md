## Direct Answer
A US freelance developer charges $40–120/hour; a software agency $80–200/hour. Freelancers win for projects under $20,000, short MVPs, and very specific skills. Agencies win for projects above $40,000, multi-component systems, continuity guarantees, and cross-functional teams. The biggest freelancer risk is bus factor of 1. The biggest agency risk is over-engineering and long timelines.
