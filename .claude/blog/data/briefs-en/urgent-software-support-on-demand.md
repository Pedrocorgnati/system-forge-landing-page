## Direct Answer (first 100 words)
Urgent software support in 2026 falls into three buckets: (1) ad-hoc on-demand developer at $150-400/hour with a 1-3 hour response window through vetted networks; (2) monthly retainer at $1,500-6,000/month with a defined SLA and named engineer; (3) original vendor's emergency line, often double the regular rate plus a contractual SLA you may not have. The fastest path to a real fix is sending a structured first message: exact error, last working state, what changed in the last 48 hours, screenshots, environment, urgency level and budget cap. Skipping this step adds 1-3 wasted hours.
