## Reader Objections
1. "Building from scratch is risky — what if it goes wrong?" → Explain the MVP methodology: launch a minimal version first (pipeline + contacts + dashboard), validate with your real team, then iterate. Risk is controlled through incremental delivery.
2. "Salesforce/HubSpot have millions of users — they must be better than something built just for me" → Acknowledge that mature SaaS platforms have a robust ecosystem. But a US SMB with 10-50 salespeople pays for features they never use. A custom CRM has exactly what you need, nothing more.
3. "What if the development company disappears?" → The code belongs to the client (contract). Standard stack (React, Node.js, PostgreSQL) = any competent developer can maintain it. Require documentation and GitHub repo access in the client's own account.
