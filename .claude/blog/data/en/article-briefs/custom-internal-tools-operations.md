Intent: informational/commercial. Mid-funnel: they know the problem, exploring solutions. Persona: operations-focused, 30–50 years old, manages a team of 10–100 people, non-technical but understands workflows.

## Required Content
- **Direct answer in first 100 words:** A custom internal tool is a software application built specifically for your team's workflow — an order management dashboard, an employee portal, a reporting system — instead of adapting a generic SaaS product to fit your processes. The best time to build one is when you're spending more than $12,000/year on SaaS tools for that function, or when your team spends more than 5 hours/week on manual data transfer between tools. Custom internal tools typically cost $15,000–$60,000 to build and pay for themselves within 18–36 months.
