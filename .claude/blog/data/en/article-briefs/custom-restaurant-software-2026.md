## FAQ (min 5 questions in natural English)

1. **Is it worth building a custom ordering app instead of using DoorDash?**
   For restaurants doing consistent delivery volume, yes — usually within 12–24 months. The math depends on your average order value and monthly volume. If you're paying $5,000–$15,000/month in commissions, a $25,000–$35,000 custom ordering system has a clear payback period. The added benefit is customer data ownership — your customers, your email list, your loyalty data.

2. **Can I keep using Toast or Square and still add custom features?**
   Yes. Most custom restaurant software projects integrate *with* existing POS systems rather than replacing them. Toast and Square have developer APIs that allow custom ordering apps, loyalty programs, and reporting tools to connect to your existing POS. A full POS replacement is a bigger investment and usually only makes sense for chains with very specific requirements.

3. **How long does it take to build a restaurant ordering app?**
   A basic web-based ordering system: 8–12 weeks. A native iOS + Android app with loyalty features: 16–24 weeks. A full platform with POS integration, delivery dispatch, and analytics: 6–9 months. Most restaurant operators launch a web ordering system first and add the mobile app in a second phase.

4. **What features do customers actually use in restaurant apps?**
   The highest-usage features are: saved orders ("reorder my usual"), loyalty points balance and rewards redemption, real-time order status tracking, and digital receipts. Features with lower usage than expected: table reservations (better handled by OpenTable/Resy), split bill functionality, and social sharing. Build for what customers use, not what sounds impressive.

5. **Do I need an app or is a website enough?**
   A mobile-optimized website with an integrated ordering flow is enough for most restaurants starting out. Native apps (iOS/Android) add features like push notifications, offline menu browsing, and Apple/Google Pay — but they also cost 50–100% more to build and require app store management. A good rule of thumb: start with a web ordering system, add the app when you have $3,000+ in monthly online orders.

6. **How does custom loyalty software compare to apps like Stamp Me or Belly?**
   Third-party loyalty apps cost $50–$200/month but lock your customer data in their platform and put their branding on your customer-facing experience. Custom loyalty software is a one-time build ($10k–$25k) that gives you full ownership of customer data, custom rewards rules, and seamless integration with your ordering system. The break-even is typically 5–15 months.
