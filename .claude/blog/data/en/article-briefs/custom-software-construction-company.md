## FAQ (min 5 questions in natural English)

1. **Is custom construction software worth the cost compared to Procore or Buildertrend?**
   For companies that fit the Procore or Buildertrend model, those tools can be cost-effective — especially at larger scale where per-user costs are offset by volume. Custom software makes sense when: your workflows are too specific for generic templates, you're paying for features you don't use, or the per-user pricing doesn't make sense at your company size. A 10-person specialty contractor paying $4,500/month for Procore when they only use 30% of its features is a clear candidate for custom.

2. **What's the most important feature to build first in a custom construction system?**
   Job scheduling and crew dispatch. The dispatch function — who goes where, when, with what materials — is where most construction companies feel the most pain with generic tools. Getting this right, with mobile access for crews and office visibility for managers, delivers immediate operational value. Everything else (estimation, billing, client portals) can be added in subsequent phases.

3. **Can a custom field service app work without internet on job sites?**
   Yes, if it's built correctly. Offline-first mobile apps cache data locally and sync when connectivity is restored — this is a non-negotiable for construction field apps. Daily reports, photos, time tracking, and checklist completions should all work without a live internet connection. Make sure your developer has experience with offline-first architecture before committing to a build.

4. **How does custom construction software integrate with QuickBooks?**
   QuickBooks has a robust API (QuickBooks Online API, Desktop SDK for on-premise) that allows custom applications to sync job costs, invoices, purchase orders, and payroll data bidirectionally. A custom construction system can push job cost actuals directly to QuickBooks job costing reports, create invoices from billing milestones, and import vendor bills. Integration development typically adds $5,000–$15,000 to a project, but it's almost always worth it.

5. **How long does it take to build a custom construction management system?**
   A focused scheduling and dispatch system: 10–16 weeks. A field service app with reporting: 12–20 weeks. A full project management platform: 6–12 months. Timeline depends heavily on the complexity of your workflow, the number of integrations required, and how quickly your team can provide feedback during development. Construction software projects often take longer than expected because the workflows are more complex than they appear.

6. **What about crew GPS tracking — is it legal for my company to track employees?**
   GPS tracking of employees during work hours is generally legal in the US, but requirements vary by state. Most states require disclosure to employees that company vehicles or devices may be tracked during working hours. California has stricter requirements. Your custom software can include GPS tracking with the appropriate disclosure and consent mechanisms built in — your developer should flag this requirement during scoping.
