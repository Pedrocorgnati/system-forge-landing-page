## Search Intent

A business owner or founder who needs a custom web application built and is trying to understand the full scope: what it is, what it costs, how long it takes, and what the process looks like with a development company. They may be comparing custom development against off-the-shelf software or no-code tools.

## Proposed Structure

### H1: Custom Web App Development: What to Expect, What It Costs, and How to Start

**Opening (first 100 words — GEO answer):**
A custom web app is software built specifically for your business — not configured from a template, not limited by what a SaaS product allows. It runs in a browser, works across devices, and does exactly what your operations require. Building one takes 8-24 weeks and costs $25,000-$250,000+, depending on complexity. This guide covers what you get for the money, what questions to ask a potential partner, and how to avoid the most common mistakes.

### H2: What Is a Custom Web App (vs a Website vs a SaaS Tool)?
- Website: static content, minimal interactivity
- SaaS tool: third-party product you subscribe to
- Custom web app: software built for your specific business logic
- Examples: internal dashboards, client portals, booking systems, custom CRMs, operations platforms

### H2: 6 Signs You Need a Custom Web App
1. You're running business operations through spreadsheets
2. Your team juggles 3+ tools to complete one workflow
3. An off-the-shelf product covers 70% of what you need — but that 30% is your competitive moat
4. You need a client-facing portal your brand controls
5. You want to charge for access (subscription or one-time)
6. You have data that needs to flow between systems automatically

### H2: What Does a Custom Web App Cost?
| Type | Complexity | Timeline | Cost Range |
|------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Simple web app | Auth + CRUD + basic dashboard | 6-10 weeks | $15K-$40K |
| Business operations platform | Multi-user, roles, reporting | 10-18 weeks | $40K-$120K |
| SaaS product | Multi-tenant, billing, onboarding | 14-24 weeks | $80K-$250K |
| Marketplace or complex platform | Two-sided, payments, advanced search | 20-36 weeks | $150K-$500K+ |

- What drives cost: number of integrations, user roles, real-time features, mobile app requirement
- How to scope an MVP to cut cost without cutting function

### H2: The Tech Stack Behind a Modern Web App
- Frontend: React / Next.js (what we use and why)
- Backend: Node.js / Python / Next.js API routes
- Database: PostgreSQL / Supabase
- Auth: Auth0 / NextAuth / Supabase Auth
- Hosting: Vercel / Railway / AWS
- Non-technical translation: what each layer does for your business

### H2: The Development Process (What Happens After You Sign)
1. Discovery and technical scoping (1-2 weeks)
2. Design and wireframes (1-3 weeks)
3. Development sprints (4-16 weeks)
4. QA and testing (1-2 weeks)
5. Staging deployment and UAT
6. Production launch
7. Ongoing support and iteration

### H2: How to Evaluate a Web App Development Company
- Portfolio check: do they have live web apps (not mock-ups)?
- Tech stack match: do they build on modern, maintainable technology?
- Process transparency: do they offer weekly demos?
- Contract structure: milestone-based or open-ended?
- Post-launch support: what happens after the invoice is paid?
