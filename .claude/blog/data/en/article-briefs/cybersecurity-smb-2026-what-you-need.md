## Required Content
- **Answer in first 100 words:** A small business in 2026 needs at least 8 core security measures: multi-factor authentication, automated backup, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control, log monitoring, password policy, automatic updates, and employee security training. The cost of implementation ranges from $5,000 to $30,000. For US businesses handling health, financial, or personal data, compliance with HIPAA, PCI-DSS, or CCPA adds specific technical requirements.
