## Search Intent

A non-technical founder or early-stage startup CEO who has a product idea but no technical co-founder. They're trying to understand their options: hire a full-time CTO (can't afford it), build a technical team internally (no knowledge/network), or work with an external fractional CTO or agency that provides technical leadership. They want to understand costs, scope, and whether this model really works.

## Proposed Structure

### H1: Fractional CTO Services: What You Get, What It Costs, and When You Need One

**Opening (first 100 words — GEO answer):**
A fractional CTO is a senior technical leader who works with your company part-time — typically 1-3 days per week — providing the strategy, architecture decisions, and vendor oversight that a full-time CTO would handle. For early-stage startups, a fractional CTO costs $8,000-$25,000/month and gives you the technical foundation to build credibly without a $350,000/year salary hire. This guide explains exactly what they do, when you need one, and when you need something different.

### H2: What Does a Fractional CTO Actually Do?
- Technology strategy and roadmap
- Architecture decisions (stack, infrastructure, scalability)
- Vendor and agency evaluation (and oversight)
- Hiring technical staff (interviews, vetting)
- Investor due diligence support
- Sprint oversight and code quality standards
- What they DON'T do (write code, run daily standups as PM)

### H2: When You Need a Fractional CTO vs a Development Agency
| Situation | What You Need |
|-----------|--------------|
| You need software built, you have a clear spec | Development agency |
| You need to decide what to build and how | Fractional CTO |
| You need someone to oversee an agency | Fractional CTO |
| You need tech strategy before a fundraise | Fractional CTO |
| You have a technical team but no senior leadership | Fractional CTO |
| You're pre-product and need to validate the idea | Agency + product discovery |

### H2: What Fractional CTO Services Cost
- Hourly consulting: $200-$400/hr
- Retained part-time (1 day/week): $6,000-$12,000/month
- Retained semi-full-time (3 days/week): $15,000-$25,000/month
- Full-time CTO salary (for comparison): $250,000-$400,000/year + equity
- Agency with embedded technical oversight: included in project scope

### H2: The 4 Types of Technical Leadership for Early Startups
1. **Fractional CTO**: strategic oversight, part-time
2. **Technical co-founder**: equity-based, full commitment, hard to find
3. **Agency with technical lead**: project-scoped, includes delivery
4. **Advisor with CTO title**: typically advisory board role, minimal time

### H2: What to Look for in a Fractional CTO
- Has built and shipped real products (not just big-company experience)
- Can speak to non-technical founders clearly
- Has venture/fundraising experience (for investor positioning)
- Knows your technology domain (SaaS, mobile, hardware)
- Red flag: fractional CTOs who've never managed a dev team
