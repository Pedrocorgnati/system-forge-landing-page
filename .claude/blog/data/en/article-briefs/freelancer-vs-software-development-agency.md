## Search Intent

A founder or business owner who needs software built and is weighing the classic dilemma: hire a freelancer (cheap, fast to start, risky) vs hire an agency (more expensive, more process, more accountability). Intent: informational with strong commercial component. They're mid-funnel: already decided to build, now deciding how. Persona: non-technical founder 30–50 years old, budget-conscious but burned by a failed freelancer project before, or new to hiring software developers.

## Required Content
- **Direct answer in first 100 words:** Hire a freelancer when your project is simple (under $10,000), well-defined, and you have technical knowledge to manage them. Hire an agency when your project is complex, involves multiple integrations, requires a team, or you need accountability and someone to manage the process for you. The price difference is real: freelancers charge $50–$150/hr vs agencies at $100–$250/hr — but the failure rate for freelance projects over $25,000 is significantly higher without proper management structures.
- **Real data:** freelance dev rates (Upwork $30–$80/hr, Toptal $100–$200/hr, local US $80–$150/hr); agency rates ($100–$250/hr); project abandonment statistics (Standish CHAOS Report: 66% of software projects are late, over budget, or cancelled); cost of project failure for SMBs
- **Concrete examples:** e-commerce founder hired Upwork freelancer for $8,000 custom plugin — abandoned at 60% completion; agency finished same scope for $15,000 in 8 weeks; SaaS startup used fractional CTO ($3,000/month) + 2 freelancers for MVP successfully
- **Comparisons:** freelancer vs agency vs fractional CTO + freelancer hybrid vs full-time employee — matrix by project size and complexity
- **FAQ:** min 5 questions on which to choose, how to verify quality, contracts, IP ownership, what happens if they leave mid-project
