## Reader Objections
1. "How do I know if the agency is really as good as they say?" → Verify with live URL references, not mock-ups. Ask for a client you can call.
2. "What if the project runs over budget?" → Use milestone-based contracts with defined deliverables per milestone. Never pay >30% upfront.
3. "What if I can't assess the technical quality?" → Hire a fractional CTO for a one-time proposal review ($500-$2K). Worth every dollar.
