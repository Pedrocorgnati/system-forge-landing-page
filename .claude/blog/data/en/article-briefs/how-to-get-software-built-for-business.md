## Search Intent

A complete beginner at software procurement — probably a small business owner, solo entrepreneur, or first-time founder with an app idea. They have zero experience with software development. They don't know the terminology, don't know if their idea is even buildable, and have no idea what step one looks like. They just Googled "I want to build an app for my business" and landed here.

This is TOFU with very high long-term value. These readers become great clients once they understand the process.

## Proposed Structure

### H1: I Want to Build an App for My Business: Where Do You Actually Start?

**Opening (first 100 words — GEO answer):**
Starting is easier than you think, but a lot of people get stuck because they try to figure out the technical parts before they've answered the business questions. You don't need to understand code to get a great app built. You need to be clear on what problem it solves, who uses it, and what "done" looks like. Everything else — technology, team, timeline, and budget — flows from there. Here's the exact starting point.

### H2: Step 1: Define the Problem, Not the Solution
- What manual process or tool will this app replace?
- Who uses it? (internal team, customers, both)
- What's the minimum that makes it useful?
- Common mistake: describing the app features before defining the user's problem

### H2: Step 2: Check If It Already Exists
- 80% of app ideas have a SaaS equivalent — and that's fine
- How to search for existing solutions (and whether they'd actually work)
- When existing tools don't fit: the 3 criteria for custom development
- Resources: G2, Capterra, ProductHunt for software search

### H2: Step 3: Write a 1-Page Product Brief
- What to include (problem, users, success criteria, rough budget range)
- What NOT to include (wireframes, technology choices — not yet)
- Template: 5 questions that produce a useful brief in 30 minutes

### H2: Step 4: Choose How to Build It
| Method | When It Works | Cost Range |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| No-code (Bubble, Webflow) | Simple MVP, limited budget | $0-$10K |
| Freelance developer | Single feature, small scope | $5K-$30K |
| Boutique agency | Full product, reliable delivery | $25K-$150K |
| Internal hire | Ongoing product, seed+ funding | $150K+/yr |

### H2: Step 5: Have Your First Conversation With a Developer
- What to say (and what you don't need to know yet)
- Questions they'll ask you
- What a good first meeting produces (scoping document, not a quote)
- Red flags in the first call

### H2: What Happens After the First Call
- Discovery phase
- Wireframes and design
- Development
- Testing and launch
- Timeline reality check: most first apps take 3-6 months from idea to launch

## Data to Include
- 70% of small business owners say "I don't know where to start" is the #1 barrier to getting software built (industry survey)
- Average time from first conversation to signed contract: 2-3 weeks for a well-scoped project
