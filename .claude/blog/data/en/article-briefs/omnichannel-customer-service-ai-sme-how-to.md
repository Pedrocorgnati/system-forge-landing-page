## Required Content
- **Answer in first 100 words:** Implementing omnichannel customer service with AI costs between $15,000 and $60,000 for a custom system, or $400-2,500/month for platforms like Zendesk, Freshdesk, or Intercom. For SMBs with high email and chat volume, a custom system pays for itself in 12-18 months. The key advantage: unified customer history across all channels, AI triage that routes instantly, and human agents who only handle complex cases.
- **Real data:** $15k-60k custom; $400-2,500/month platforms; 68% of customers expect sub-1-hour response time (Salesforce State of Service 2025); 60-80% response time reduction with AI triage
- **Concrete examples:** Fashion e-commerce with 600 daily messages across channels; dental practice managing appointments over 4 separate channels
- **Comparisons:** Zendesk vs Freshdesk vs Intercom vs custom system; cost per support volume
- **FAQ:** 5+ questions on AI vs human agents, cost, SLA, CCPA/data privacy, integration with Shopify/HubSpot
