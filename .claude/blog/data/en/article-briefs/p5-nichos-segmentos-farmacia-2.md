## Conteudo Obrigatorio
- **Resposta nos primeiros 100 palavras:** In the United States, a pharmacy mobile app typically costs between $15,000 and $150,000. Simple apps (white-label or basic catalog) start at $15,000–$30,000. Functional MVPs with delivery, payments, and pharmacy system integration range from $40,000 to $90,000. Robust solutions for chains or advanced features (telepharmacy, AI, automation) can exceed $130,000. Final pricing depends on scope, design, integrations, and regulatory compliance.
- **Dados reais:**
  - Basic MVP: $15,000–$30,000
  - Functional MVP with delivery + payments: $40,000–$90,000
  - Enterprise solution for pharmacy chains: $100,000–$150,000+
  - Monthly maintenance: 10–20% of project value
  - Average delivery timeline: 6–14 weeks
- **Exemplos concretos:**
  - Single-location neighborhood pharmacy: catalog + messaging + simple ordering, delivered in 8 weeks, investment of $20,000
  - Regional chain (5 stores): delivery tracking, integrated inventory, and loyalty program, delivered in 12 weeks, investment of $75,000
  - Pharmacy with telepharmacy: app with online consultations and e-prescriptions, delivered in 16 weeks, investment of $125,000
- **Comparacoes:**
  - Custom app: higher upfront investment, full control, better long-term margins
  - Marketplace (e.g., Amazon Pharmacy, CVS app): low entry cost, high per-sale fees, less brand control
  - White-label: fast deployment and lower cost, but limited customization and scalability
