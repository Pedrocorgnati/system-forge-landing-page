## Search Intent

A non-technical founder or small business owner who has a software product idea and needs to understand the full journey: what building SaaS actually involves, what it costs, how long it takes, and whether they need an agency or could use no-code tools. They want permission to build and a practical roadmap to follow.

Secondary intent: validate that building custom SaaS is the right move (vs buying existing software) and understand what to look for in a development partner.

Building a SaaS product from scratch takes 3 to 12 months and $30,000 to $300,000+, depending on complexity. The three decisions that determine which end of that range you land on: how tightly scoped your MVP is, whether you build multi-tenant from day one, and who builds it. This guide walks you through every stage — from idea validation to first paying customer — written for founders who don't code.
