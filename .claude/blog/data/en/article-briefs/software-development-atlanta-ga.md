2. **Why is Atlanta such a strong market for fintech software?**
   Atlanta processes a disproportionate share of US payment card transactions — NCR, Global Payments, and Worldpay are all headquartered in the metro area. This has created a dense ecosystem of fintech talent, financial services companies, and software vendors who understand payment processing, KYC/AML compliance, and banking integrations. If you're building fintech software in the Southeast, Atlanta is the natural hub.

3. **Does my Atlanta fintech company need special compliance in its software?**
   Yes, likely. If you're processing payments, you need PCI DSS compliance. If you're doing KYC/AML for financial accounts, you need FinCEN compliance and appropriate watchlist screening. If you're lending, you need CFPB-compliant disclosure flows. These requirements affect how your software is built, not just what it does — your development partner needs to understand them from the start.
