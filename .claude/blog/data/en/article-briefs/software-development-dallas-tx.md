## FAQ (min 5 questions in natural English)

1. **What does custom software development cost in Dallas?**
   In Dallas-Fort Worth, local agency rates run $100–$200/hour. A typical business web application costs $25,000–$80,000 all-in, with mobile apps adding $35,000–$100,000. If you work with a nearshore partner (same CST timezone, competitive rates), you can expect similar quality at 40–55% lower cost. Project complexity, integrations, and your timeline all affect the final number.

2. **Do I need to hire a local Dallas developer?**
   Not necessarily. Modern development projects are built collaboratively — code reviews, standups, and communication happen online regardless of geography. What matters is timezone alignment (so you're not waiting overnight for answers), communication quality, and technical fit. A nearshore team in the same CST timezone offers both local availability and competitive pricing.

3. **What industries does SystemForge serve in DFW?**
   Healthcare technology (HIPAA-compliant apps), logistics and supply chain software, real estate platforms, financial services, and general business automation. DFW's economy is heavily weighted toward these sectors, and we have experience building software that fits their specific compliance and integration requirements.

4. **How long does a custom software project take for a DFW company?**
   A focused web application: 8–16 weeks. A mobile app: 14–24 weeks. A full-featured business platform: 4–9 months. Timeline depends on your requirements, how quickly your team can provide feedback, and how many integrations you need. Projects with clear requirements and engaged stakeholders consistently ship faster.

5. **What's the best way to evaluate a software development vendor in Dallas?**
   Request a portfolio with references from similar industries. Have a technical conversation with the actual developers (not just salespeople). Ask about their process for managing scope changes and timeline delays. Review their contract for IP ownership (you should own all code), payment structure (milestone-based, not all upfront), and what happens if the project stalls.
