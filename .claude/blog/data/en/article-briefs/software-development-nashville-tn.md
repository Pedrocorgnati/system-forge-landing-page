## FAQ (min 5 questions in natural English)

1. **What does custom software development cost in Nashville?**
   Local Nashville agencies typically charge $85–$165/hour. A standard web application runs $25,000–$80,000; HIPAA-compliant healthcare applications add 20–30% for compliance architecture. A nearshore partner in the same CST timezone costs $40–$85/hour. Your specific cost depends on your scope, healthcare compliance requirements, and integration needs with existing systems.

2. **Why is Nashville such a strong market for healthcare software?**
   Nashville has more healthcare management companies per capita than almost any US city. HCA Healthcare, Optum, Change Healthcare, and hundreds of smaller healthcare service companies are all headquartered here. This concentration has created strong demand for healthcare operations software, revenue cycle management tools, patient engagement platforms, and HIPAA-compliant data systems. Any software company working with Nashville healthcare clients needs genuine HIPAA compliance experience.

3. **Does my Nashville healthcare company need HIPAA-compliant software?**
   If you handle protected health information (PHI) — whether as a covered entity or as a business associate — yes. HIPAA compliance affects your software architecture: encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access controls, audit logging, Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with your software vendor, and breach notification procedures. A developer without HIPAA experience will miss these requirements; a developer with it will build them in from day one.

4. **What hospitality and events software do Nashville companies typically need?**
   Nashville's hospitality sector — driven by tourism, live music, and events — creates demand for: venue booking and availability management, ticketing and registration platforms, event logistics coordination tools, and restaurant/bar management systems. These typically run $15,000–$40,000 for focused solutions and $40,000–$80,000 for full-featured platforms.

5. **How quickly can a software development project start for a Nashville company?**
   With a nearshore partner in the CST timezone, projects typically start within 2–4 weeks of contract signing. You get real-time availability during Nashville business hours — standups, Slack, and code reviews happen on your schedule, not overnight.
