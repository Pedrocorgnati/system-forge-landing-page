## FAQ (minimum 5)

1. **What does software development actually cost in Phoenix, AZ in 2026?** Senior US engineers via Phoenix-area agencies bill $80–$220/hour, vs. $150–$400/hour in SF/NYC and $40–$90/hour nearshore. Typical projects: focused custom web tool $25–80K, multi-module platform $80–250K, mobile app $40–150K, custom SaaS $150K–$1M+. Add 15–30% of build cost annually for maintenance.

2. **Should I hire a local Phoenix agency or a remote US software house?** Local on-site Phoenix agencies are strong for tight collaboration, defense/regulated work where the partner must embed in your office, and clients who strongly prefer in-person sprints. Remote US partners cover most standard builds (custom web, mobile, SaaS) at lower overhead, with on-site visits scheduled for kickoff and key milestones. Time zone overlap with MST is excellent from any US time zone.

3. **What industries does Phoenix's software ecosystem serve best?** Healthcare (Banner Health, HonorHealth, Mayo Clinic Arizona), fintech and banking, semiconductor supply chain (TSMC's Phoenix fab is now operational), hospitality (ChoiceHotels and Best Western HQs), real estate (residential + commercial), and consumer tech (Axon, Carvana, GoDaddy). Each has distinct compliance and integration patterns that an experienced partner should already know.

4. **How does nearshore (LATAM) compare to a US-based partner for a Phoenix client?** Nearshore rates ($40–$90/hour) are materially lower, but the tradeoffs are real: more PM overhead on your side, async/Slack-heavy collaboration, more rigorous documentation needed, accountability gaps without strong in-house product management. For Phoenix clients with strong internal PM and well-defined scope, nearshore can work. For first-time custom builds or complex regulated work, the US partner usually pencils out better despite the higher rate.

5. **Can a remote US partner really do on-site visits in Phoenix?** Yes, easily. PHX is a major hub (American, Southwest, United nonstops from most US cities). Most remote US software houses budget 1–3 on-site visits per project (kickoff, mid-build milestone, launch) without dramatically affecting total cost. For ongoing engagements, monthly or quarterly visits are typical and explicitly priced.

6. **How long do typical Phoenix software projects take?** Marketing site + CMS: 4–10 weeks. Custom web application: 12–22 weeks. Multi-module business platform: 6–14 months. Mobile app (iOS + Android): 14–26 weeks. Custom SaaS / multi-tenant: 9–18+ months. Add 4–8 weeks for discovery and scoping before build kickoff.

7. **What should I look for in a software partner serving Phoenix?** Six things in order: (1) industry experience matching yours (healthcare, fintech, real estate, hospitality, etc.), (2) US-based engineers with clear ownership (avoid shops that resell to offshore without disclosure), (3) transparent USD pricing and statement of work, (4) compliance awareness (HIPAA, PCI, ADA, SOC 2 if applicable), (5) on-site visit cadence agreed in writing, (6) post-launch support model (retainer or T&M with response SLAs). Avoid the "we do everything" pitch — depth matters.
