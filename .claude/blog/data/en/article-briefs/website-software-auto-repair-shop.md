# Brief: Auto Repair Shop Website + Booking, Estimates, SMS Status — Real Costs (2026)

## Editorial Goal

Move auto repair (independent shops, tire shops, transmission, body shops) website content from "trust badges + services" to a **repair order lifecycle system**: VIN-aware appointment booking, digital vehicle inspection (DVI) report delivery, estimate approval via text link, SMS status updates ("waiting for part," "in test drive," "ready for pickup"), Carfax service history integration, and TCPA-compliant SMS consent. Differentiator: dollarize the value of an approved upsell ($60–280 average ticket lift on DVI-driven repairs), name the shop management systems (Shopmonkey, Tekmetric, Mitchell1, ALLDATA Manage, Protractor, ROWriter), and explain when off-the-shelf wins vs. custom. Persona: independent shop owner or multi-bay operator (1–6 locations).

## Target Persona

- **Primary:** Owner of an independent US auto repair shop (3–12 bays), tire shop, transmission specialist, or body shop running on Shopmonkey, Tekmetric, Mitchell1 Manage SE, ALLDATA Manage, Protractor, or ROWriter.
- **Secondary:** Operator of a small multi-location chain (3–10 locations) needing standardized branding + central scheduling.
- **Typical trigger:** "Customers don't know what's happening with their car for 4 hours — we lose trust" / "Our DVI photos and estimates email but customers don't open email — we want SMS approval" / "We just switched from Mitchell1 to Shopmonkey and our website doesn't sync."

## Direct Answer (GEO — first 100 words)

An auto repair shop website with online booking, VIN capture, DVI approval flow, and SMS status updates costs **$4,000–$22,000** to build in the US in 2026: a themed site with embedded shop SaaS booking (Shopmonkey, Tekmetric) lands $4–9K + $200–500/month; a custom site with deeper integration, branded DVI delivery, and SMS marketing runs $10–22K + $300–700/month. Most shops should run their booking on Shopmonkey or Tekmetric (off-the-shelf) and customize only the marketing site. Shops with proper SMS status updates + DVI approval lift average repair order by **$60–280** through approved upsells. ROI window: **3–8 months** for a shop doing 15+ ROs/day.

## FAQ (minimum 5)

1. **How much does an auto repair shop website with online booking really cost in 2026?** $4,000–$22,000 to build. Themed site with embedded shop SaaS booking (Shopmonkey, Tekmetric): $4–9K + $200–500/month. Custom site with VIN capture + DVI link + SMS marketing: $10–22K + $300–700/month. Multi-location chains start at $25K+. Monthly stack typically: shop SaaS subscription, SMS, payment processing, reviews.

2. **Should I use Shopmonkey, Tekmetric, Mitchell1, or build custom?** For most independent shops (3–12 bays), Shopmonkey or Tekmetric is the right call — modern UX, DVI built-in, two-way SMS, payment processing native, $169–399/month. Mitchell1 Manage SE remains strong for shops deep in OEM repair data. Custom is justified only for multi-location chains, specialty shops with unusual workflows, or sellable SaaS plays. Building custom RO/SMS software below $1M revenue almost never pays back.

3. **Will SMS status updates and DVI approval really lift my average RO?** Yes, materially. Shops with proper DVI (photos + line-item approval via SMS link) and consistent SMS status updates see average RO lift of $60–280 from approved upsells. The mechanism: visual evidence (photos of the leaking gasket, worn brake pad) creates buyer confidence; SMS approval link reduces friction vs. phone tag; one-click line-item approval lets customers approve some items and decline others. For a 15-RO/day shop, that's roughly $900–4,200/day in incremental approved work.

4. **What is TCPA and why does my SMS workflow need it?** TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act) regulates business SMS to consumers. You need: (1) written or e-signed opt-in at intake (RO acceptance language is fine if it explicitly mentions SMS), (2) clear opt-out language in messages ("Reply STOP to opt out"), (3) A2P 10DLC registration via your SMS provider (Twilio, ShopGenie, AutoVitals SmartFlow handle this). Violations carry $500–$1,500 per text in statutory damages. Class actions against repair shops do happen.

5. **Do I need VIN capture on the website?** Strongly recommended. VIN auto-populates year/make/model/trim/engine (saves 5–10 min per intake), enables accurate service-time estimates, surfaces open NHTSA recalls, and feeds Carfax service history integrations. Modern shop SaaS (Shopmonkey, Tekmetric) handle camera VIN scan at intake; your website's booking widget should accept VIN input and pre-fill the visit data.

6. **What's the realistic conversion rate for a repair shop website?** 2–4% for typical shops, 4–8% for well-tuned shops with strong local SEO + reviews + booking widget + financing offer. Major conversion lifters: persistent booking CTA, Google reviews visible above fold, service-specific pages (not one mega "services" page), financing badge (Synchrony, Sunbit, Affirm) for repairs over $500. Forced account creation kills conversion — guest booking with vehicle/VIN input is the right pattern.

7. **How long does the project take?** Themed site with embedded shop SaaS booking: 3–6 weeks. Custom site with VIN capture + DVI integration + SMS marketing: 10–16 weeks. Multi-location chain with central scheduling + branded portal: 14–22 weeks. Shop SaaS API certification (especially Mitchell1 and Shopmonkey) can add 1–3 weeks. Photography of the shop + techs takes 1–2 weeks to schedule and produce — start early.
