A dental practice website with online scheduling, HIPAA-compliant intake forms, and automated SMS confirmations costs **$5,000–$25,000** to build, depending on scope: a clean themed site with embedded third-party scheduler (NexHealth, LocalMed, Adit, Weave) lands $5–10K; a custom site with deeper PMS integration (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental), insurance eligibility checks, and patient portal runs $15–25K. ROI is operations-driven — practices reduce no-shows from 12–18% baseline to **4–7%**, recovering $800–3,500/month in a typical 2-chair practice, with breakeven inside 4–9 months.

## FAQ (minimum 5)

1. **How much does a dental practice website with online scheduling cost in 2026?** $5,000–$25,000 to build, plus $200–700/month for hosting, scheduler subscription, SMS, and PMS sync. Themed site with embedded scheduler: $5–10K. Custom + PMS sync + automated recall: $15–25K. DSO multi-location bases start at $30K.

2. **Will online scheduling actually reduce no-shows?** Yes, materially. Industry baseline no-show is 12–18% for typical GP practices; well-tuned automation (confirmation SMS at 48h + 24h + 2h, easy reschedule link, online deposit for high-value appointments) drops this to 4–7%. For a 2-chair practice, that recovers $800–3,500/month — usually paying for the whole stack within 4–9 months.

3. **Is my dental website covered by HIPAA?** The marketing surface (services, location, team bios, blog) is not PHI territory. The contact form is borderline — keep it to name/email/phone, no clinical history. Anything clinical (medical questionnaire, X-rays, treatment plan, intra-oral photos) lives in the patient portal under a BAA with the PMS or portal vendor. HHS issued December 2024 guidance restricting third-party tracking pixels on pages that could imply a health condition — many practices removed Meta Pixel after this.

4. **Should I use Weave, Adit, NexHealth, or build custom?** For most 1–4 op practices, an off-the-shelf scheduler (NexHealth or LocalMed for Dentrix/Eaglesoft, Adit or Weave for all-in-one) is the right call — they're HIPAA-compliant, PMS-synced, and battle-tested. Custom is justified for DSOs (5+ locations, branded consistency), specialty practices with unusual workflows, or B2B-adjacent services (membership plans, wholesale). Be honest with yourself: 80% of practices belong in the off-the-shelf bucket.

5. **What's the deal with insurance verification on the website?** "We accept your insurance" buttons are misleading because acceptance ≠ in-network status ≠ specific coverage. The real solution is real-time eligibility via DentalXChange or pVerify — patient enters insurance info, the system pulls actual benefits, and the front office gets a clean snapshot before the visit. This saves 15–25 minutes per intake and prevents the awkward "your coverage doesn't include this" conversation in the chair.

6. **Do I need a separate patient portal?** Usually yes, but you don't need to build it. The PMS vendors (Dentrix Ascend, Eaglesoft Patient Portal, Open Dental Web Sched, Curve Dental, Denticon) offer portals as add-ons. Building custom is only justified for DSOs standardizing across PMS-mixed environments or specialty practices where the off-the-shelf portal can't model the workflow.

7. **How long does the project take?** Themed site with embedded scheduler: 4–6 weeks. Custom site + PMS-synced scheduler + automation: 8–14 weeks. DSO multi-location template + custom portal: 16–24 weeks. Add 2–4 weeks if your PMS sync requires custom API work (Open Dental in particular).
