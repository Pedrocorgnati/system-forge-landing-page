## FAQ (minimum 5)

1. **How much does an independent pharmacy website really cost in 2026?** $6,000–$30,000 to build. Themed site with embedded refill widget (Digital Pharmacist, RxLocal): $6–12K + $200–600/month. Custom site with PMS sync + delivery + OTC ecommerce: $15–25K + $300–900/month. Multi-location chains start at $30K+. Monthly stack typically includes hosting, refill platform, SMS, delivery platform.

2. **Will online refill ordering actually retain more patients?** Yes, materially. Independents typically see 2–5% lift in refill retention with a working online refill flow + SMS reminders + delivery, vs. patients who transfer to CVS/Walgreens/Amazon for convenience. At $30–80 gross margin per retained Rx per year, a 1,500-script pharmacy retaining 30–75 extra patients/year recovers $900–6,000/month — usually paying for the entire stack within 6–14 months.

3. **What about HIPAA for pharmacy websites?** Marketing surface (services, location, team) is not PHI. The refill flow IS PHI (name + Rx number + medication). Every vendor touching this data — refill platform, SMS provider, web host, analytics — needs a signed BAA. HHS issued December 2024 guidance restricting third-party tracking pixels (Meta, Google Analytics) on pages that could imply a health condition. Most independent pharmacies removed Meta Pixel from refill pages after this.

4. **Can patients order controlled substances online?** No. DEA rules require Schedule II controlled substances to be e-prescribed directly from prescriber to pharmacy (no patient-initiated online refill). Schedule III–V may have limited refill workflows but vary by state board rules. Online refill platforms restrict to non-controlled meds — make sure yours does and you don't accidentally enable a workflow your state board prohibits.

5. **Should I use Digital Pharmacist / Mobiletext / RxLocal or build custom?** For most 1–5 location independents, off-the-shelf platforms (Digital Pharmacist, Mobiletext / Mobile Pharmacy Solutions, RxLocal, Pocket Rx, ScriptPro Refill) are the right call — they're HIPAA-compliant, PMS-integrated, battle-tested, and priced $200–600/mo per location. Custom is justified for chains needing standardized multi-location branding, specialty/compounding pharmacies with unusual workflows, or sellable SaaS plays.

6. **How do I add delivery?** Three options. Self-delivery: best margin, requires van + driver + insurance + dispatching, only viable with concentrated geography. White-label (ScriptDrop, ZipDrug, DoorDash Drive, Uber Direct): you keep the patient, pay ~$7–15/delivery, no fleet overhead. Hybrid: self-deliver in tight zones, white-label for outliers. Cold-chain meds (insulin, GLP-1, biologics) need temperature-controlled handling — confirm vendor capability before launching.

7. **How long does the project take?** Themed site + embedded refill widget: 4–8 weeks. Custom site + deeper PMS integration + delivery tracking + OTC ecommerce: 10–18 weeks. Multi-location with custom portal: 16–24 weeks. PMS integrations (especially smaller PMS vendors) can add 2–4 weeks for API work and certification.
