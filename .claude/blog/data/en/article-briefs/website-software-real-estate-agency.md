## FAQ (minimum 5)

1. **How much does a real estate agency website with IDX really cost in 2026?** Themed all-in-one (Real Geeks, kvCORE, Sierra, Lofty): $5–10K setup + $300–1,500/month. Custom site + IDX widget + CRM integration: $12–25K + $200–600/month. Fully custom with RESO Web API + custom CRM: $25–35K+. Add $30–200/month per MLS for IDX/VOW licensing — not negotiable.

2. **Should I use kvCORE / Real Geeks / Sierra or build custom?** All-in-one platforms cover 70% of brokerages well, especially solos and teams under 10 agents — they're integrated, fast to launch, and their lead routing is already tuned. Custom is justified for brokerages with strong brand identity, 10+ agents, luxury/niche specialty, or proptech ambitions. Be honest with yourself about whether your brand or workflow truly requires custom.

3. **Will my own website beat Zillow Premier Agent for lead quality?** Different leads, different unit economics. Zillow leads are high-volume, low-intent (browsing buyers), cost $1,800+/month per zip in competitive areas, and convert at 0.5–1%. Your own site's leads (typically Google + SEO + paid + referrals) are lower volume, higher intent, and convert at 2–4%. Most successful teams run both — Zillow for volume awareness, own site for retention and high-intent buyers.

4. **What is RESO Web API and why does it matter?** RESO Web API is the modern REST-based MLS data standard that replaced RETS feeds. Most US MLSs migrated between 2023 and 2025. It enables faster syncs (minutes vs. hourly), richer data (more fields per listing, photos as media objects, easier filtering), and easier custom integrations. If your MLS supports RESO Web API, prefer it over RETS for any new custom build.

5. **How important is speed-to-lead really?** Empirically critical. Harvard Business Review research found leads contacted within 1 hour are 7x more likely to qualify; within 5 minutes is 21x more likely than 30+ min. In practice, this means SMS auto-response under 60s, agent-call within 5 min during business hours, and an after-hours playbook (qualified ISA service or scheduled callback). Most "we have a CRM" brokerages still respond in hours, not minutes.

6. **Do I need a separate agent microsite for each agent?** Solo top-producers — yes, brand differentiation is real. Team-of-3 to team-of-10 — usually one brokerage site + agent profile pages, not separate microsites. 20+ agent brokerages — agent microsites are recruiting tools (agents want them), but they should share IDX/CRM with the parent site. Don't fragment your data across 30 different vendors.

7. **What about Fair Housing compliance for paid ads?** HUD prohibits targeting or excluding by protected class (race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability) and many states add more. Meta's Special Ad Category for Housing limits targeting (no zip-radius below 15 mi, no age/gender, no detailed targeting). Google has similar restrictions. Get your ads reviewed by your broker's compliance officer before launching — the fines and reputational risk are real.
