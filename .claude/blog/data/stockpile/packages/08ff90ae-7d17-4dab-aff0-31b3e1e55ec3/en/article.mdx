# How Much Does a Clinic Mobile App Cost in 2025

The cost of a clinic mobile app in the United States typically ranges from $8,000 to $250,000, depending on features, integrations, security requirements, and platform choices. For most small to mid-sized clinics, the realistic range falls between $25,000 and $90,000. In this guide, I break down every pricing factor and how to build a budget that matches your actual needs.

Across 40+ software projects built for clinics and medical practices in the US, Brazil, and Europe, we have learned that the most expensive mistake is not paying too much for the app. It is paying for features that look important but do not change the patient's or the admin team's daily workflow.

## What drives the cost of a clinic mobile app

The final price depends on four pillars:

1. **Feature scope** — scheduling, medical records, telemedicine, payments, automated reminders.
2. **Integrations** — connection with EHR/EMR systems, payment APIs, and notification providers.
3. **Security and compliance** — HIPAA, GDPR, encryption, audit logs, access controls.
4. **Platforms** — iOS, Android, or both. Native apps usually cost more than cross-platform ones.

A simple app with scheduling and reminders can start at around $8,000. A solution with telemedicine, integrated records, and a full patient portal can easily exceed $100,000.

## Common mistakes — and how to avoid them

### 1. Copying a large hospital app

Smaller clinics do not need 80% of the features a hospital uses. Starting too big delays delivery and makes team adoption harder.

### 2. Ignoring integration with existing systems

Most clinics already use an EHR, EMR, or practice management system. If the app does not talk to it, staff end up entering everything twice.

### 3. Forgetting older patients

A significant share of clinic users is over 60. Simple screens, large fonts, and WhatsApp or SMS confirmations make a real difference in adoption.

### 4. Underestimating HIPAA and data security

Health data requires specific protections. Encryption, role-based access, audit logs, and proper privacy policies are not optional.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a medical clinic app cost in the US?

For most small to mid-sized clinics, between $25,000 and $90,000. Simple apps can start at $8,000, while enterprise-grade solutions can exceed $150,000.

### Is React Native cheaper for clinic app development?

Usually yes. React Native uses one codebase for both iOS and Android, typically reducing cost and timeline by 20% to 40% compared to native development.

### How long does it take to build a clinic mobile app?

A typical MVP takes 8 to 12 weeks. More complete apps with telemedicine and complex integrations usually take 14 to 22 weeks.

### What features should a clinic app have?

Online scheduling, automated appointment confirmations, a patient portal, and integration with medical records or practice management software are the highest-impact features.

### Does a clinic app need to be HIPAA compliant?

Yes. In the US, any app handling protected health information (PHI) must comply with HIPAA, including encryption, access controls, and business associate agreements when needed.

### Should we start with a mobile app or a web portal for patients?

It depends on patient habits. If most bookings already come from mobile, an app accelerates adoption. If operations are still manual, starting with a web patient portal may be a safer first step.
