# How Much Does a Mobile App for a Beauty Salon Cost?

Building a mobile app for a beauty salon in the United States typically costs between $8,000 and $45,000, depending on the salon size, features, and how you choose to develop it. In this guide, I will break down the cost factors, timelines, and what to include in scope so you do not pay for software that does not solve your problem.

Across more than 30 custom projects we have built for small businesses, I have seen salons lose clients because of disorganized booking and slow communication. A well-built app fixes that without forcing the owner to become a tech manager.

## What Drives the Price of a Beauty Salon App

The cost is not just about screens. The real price comes from the logic behind them:

- **Real-time online booking**: syncing with the salon schedule and blocking taken slots requires reliable synchronization.
- **Automated reminders**: push, WhatsApp, or SMS notifications reduce no-shows, but pricing scales with message volume.
- **In-app payments**: credit card, Apple Pay, and split payments add convenience but require a secure payment gateway.
- **Client portal**: service history, loyalty points, and rebooking increase retention.
- **Owner dashboard**: commission tracking, basic inventory, and appointment reports help run daily operations.

The more of these you include in the first version, the higher the initial investment. The good news is that you can start with a focused scope and expand later.

## How Much Each Salon App Option Costs

There are three main paths to getting a beauty salon app. The right one depends on your business size and how much control you want.

### Off-the-shelf booking apps

Generic scheduling platforms charge between $50 and $400 per month. They work early on, but they limit your brand, business rules, and integrations.

### Custom development with a freelancer

An experienced freelancer can deliver a basic app for $6,000 to $18,000. The risk is maintenance, documentation, and project continuity.

### Specialized agency or studio

A mature agency delivers salon apps between $15,000 and $45,000. That price includes defined scope, design, testing, app store submission, and post-launch support.

## Common Mistakes When Building a Salon App

**1. Trying to ship every feature at once**
This delays launch, increases cost, and often includes features clients never use.

**2. Ignoring the end-client experience**
An app that looks great for the owner but confuses the person booking it will not drive results.

**3. Not planning for maintenance**
Store updates, bug fixes, and improvements cost money after launch. Leaving this out of the budget is how apps get abandoned.

**4. Choosing only by lowest price**
Quotes far below market usually hide unclear scope, missing tests, or hard-to-reach support.

## When to Hire vs. Handle It In-House

Hiring a developer or agency makes sense when:

- Your salon serves more than 150 clients per month
- Your calendar is already bottlenecked and no-shows cost real money
- You want a branded solution, not a generic template
- You need payment, WhatsApp, or reporting integration

Handling it in-house can work if:

- Your booking volume is still low
- You or someone on your team is comfortable with a simple scheduling platform
- The goal is just to test whether clients will use online booking

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a basic beauty salon app cost?

A basic custom app with booking, reminders, and a client portal typically ranges from $8,000 to $18,000.

### Can I build a salon app cheaply?

Yes, using off-the-shelf scheduling apps. The trade-off is limited customization and business rules. For owners who want their own brand and growth, custom development pays off.

### How long does it take to build a salon app?

A functional first version takes 8 to 12 weeks, depending on scope. More complete projects with payments, commissions, and reporting can take 4 to 6 months.

### Do I need to publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, if you want clients to download it directly. Publishing requires a developer account and app store approval, which usually takes a few days.

### Will the app replace the salon's WhatsApp?

No, but it organizes the workflow. The app reduces repetitive confirmation messages and frees WhatsApp for human support and sales.
