A bakery that doesn't know its true cost per loaf, loses custom cake orders because the paper log went missing and reorders flour by gut feel is leaving margin on the table every single day. In 2026, bakeries and pastry shops face a double squeeze: ingredient costs that keep rising and customers who expect more — custom orders, online pre-ordering and consistent quality. Without a management system, the owner works hard and still doesn't know if they're actually profitable.

## What bakery management software needs to do

### Recipe costing and production planning

Every product needs a recipe card with ingredients, quantities per unit produced and cost calculated automatically. Flour price went up? The system recalculates the cost of bread, croissants and sourdough instantly. You know immediately whether your selling price still protects your margin or needs to be adjusted.

### Daily production scheduling

The system plans production quantities based on historical sales data: Mondays sell more sandwich bread, Saturdays sell more celebration cakes. The baker receives the day's production sheet with calculated quantities. Overproduction means waste; underproduction means an empty case and a lost customer.

### Custom order management

Custom birthday cake for Saturday — chocolate ganache, 20 servings, fresh flowers, inscription "Happy 50th, Karen" — this needs to be registered in the system with delivery date, customer details, specifications, deposit paid and balance owed. The system alerts Thursday morning that Saturday's cake is due and shows the exact production card.

### POS integrated with inventory

The point of sale registers every sale and automatically decrements inventory. Sold 40 blueberry muffins? The system deducts the proportional quantities of blueberries, flour, eggs and butter based on the recipe card. Ingredient inventory stays current without manual daily counts.

### Ingredient inventory and reorder management

Low-stock alerts for flour, sugar, butter and other core ingredients. Consumption history to calculate optimal reorder frequency. Automatic purchase order generation to a supplier when inventory drops below a configured minimum threshold.

### Sales and profitability reporting

Which products sell the most? Which shift is most profitable? Which item has the highest margin? Which custom orders generated the most revenue this month? The system consolidates this into simple dashboards. The owner makes decisions based on data, not instinct.

| Lightspeed Restaurant | Bakery-cafés | $200–450 |
| Toast POS | Counter-service bakeries | $150–400 |
| MarketMan | Multi-location with inventory focus | $250–600 |
| Custom build | Chains and premium bakeries | Project-based |

For a single-location bakery, Toast or Lightspeed handle the POS and basic inventory. For bakeries with a central production facility, multiple retail locations and wholesale distribution to restaurants or grocery stores, a custom system integrates what generic SaaS can't — especially the recipe costing module with real-time margin tracking.

## Online ordering and delivery for bakeries

Online bakery sales grew significantly post-pandemic and the channel is here to stay. A system that doesn't connect to DoorDash, Uber Eats or your own e-commerce loses this revenue stream. Online orders should flow directly into the production queue, decrement inventory and update financials — without the cashier re-entering anything manually.

For bakeries with their own delivery service (not via a platform), the system can include:

- **Customer app** for ordering and tracking
- **Driver route management** for deliveries
- **Mobile POS for at-door payment**
- **Scheduled delivery** for custom orders with fixed pickup dates

## The critical margin problem

Most bakery owners know their selling prices but not their costs. Without digitized recipe cards, margin is estimated by feel. When flour prices jump 20%, the owner feels it in the bank account but can't pinpoint exactly which products are underwater. With the system, a per-product margin report is available instantly — and you can adjust prices on specific items without a blanket price increase that drives customers to competitors.

## Food safety compliance: HACCP and allergen management

Bakeries supplying schools, hospitals or grocery chains need full traceability: supplier, lot number, ingredient expiration dates, production batch. The system records this and enables traceability in case of a food safety issue. Many commercial buyers now require documented HACCP compliance as a supplier qualification — this is increasingly a business necessity, not just regulatory formality.

## FAQ — Frequently asked questions about bakery software

**Can the system handle both artisan production and semi-industrial output?**
Yes. The recipe card and production planning module adapts to small-batch hand-crafted production (with variable timing and yields) and to more structured operations with semi-automated lines. Yield percentages and waste factors are configurable per product.

**How does custom order management work for wedding cakes?**
The salesperson registers the order with: cake style, filling, frosting, design notes, delivery date, customer name, total amount and deposit paid. The system generates the production card and alerts the team with a configurable lead time (e.g., 72 hours before delivery or pickup).

**Does the system integrate with DoorDash and Uber Eats?**
Direct integration depends on the solution. Custom-built systems and some SaaS platforms (like Olo) can receive orders from third-party platforms directly into the production queue without re-entry.

**How much does it cost to digitize a bakery with 5 employees?**
Basic SaaS POS: $150–250/month. Full system with integrated inventory, recipe costing and custom order management: $300–600/month. Custom-built system for a chain starts at $40,000.

**Does the system handle sales tax compliance?**
Yes. In most US states, baked goods sold for immediate consumption are taxable while packaged baked goods sold for home consumption are exempt. A properly configured POS applies the correct tax treatment automatically based on the product category.
