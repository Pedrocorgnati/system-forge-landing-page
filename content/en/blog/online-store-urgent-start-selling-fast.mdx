# Online Store Urgent: How to Launch Your E-commerce and Start Selling Fast

If you need to launch an online store urgently, you have two paths: (1) a SaaS platform (Shopify, BigCommerce, WooCommerce) to be selling in 3–7 days with a setup cost of $500–$2,000, or (2) a custom-built store with your own design and features in 2–4 weeks for $5,000–$18,000. For genuine urgency, I recommend launching on a SaaS platform first and migrating to a custom build when revenue justifies it. The goal is to start selling — not to have the perfect store.

The truth that not enough people say out loud: the best online store is the one that's selling. Everything else is future optimization.

## What to Do First When You Need an Online Store on an Urgent Timeline

Before thinking about platforms, answer these three questions:

1. **How many products are you launching with?** Under 100 products, any platform works. Over 500, you need bulk import and robust catalog management.
2. **Are you selling direct-to-consumer (DTC) or to other businesses (B2B)?** DTC is straightforward. B2B requires login, account-based pricing, minimum order quantities, and net terms — a different level of complexity.
3. **What's your expected monthly revenue in year one?** Under $50,000/month, a SaaS platform is the right call. Above that, transaction fees start eating margin and a custom build makes economic sense.

**Launch plan for an urgent online store:**

- **Days 1–2:** Platform signup, domain connection, theme selection
- **Days 3–4:** Product uploads (photos, descriptions, prices, inventory)
- **Day 5:** Payment setup (Stripe + PayPal) and shipping configuration (ShipStation or Shopify Shipping)
- **Days 6–7:** Test purchases, final adjustments, go-live

A natural cosmetics brand that needed to launch before a major holiday used exactly this timeline. Shopify store live in five days with 60 products, integrated Stripe payments, and automated shipping via Shopify Shipping. Revenue in the first month: $18,000.

### Payment Setup: Stripe, PayPal, Shopify Payments

Payment is the last place to cut corners on setup time.

- **Stripe:** Best API, excellent for subscriptions and custom checkout flows. Setup in a few hours. Transaction fee: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. Supports 135+ currencies.
- **PayPal:** Still trusted by consumers, especially for first-time buyers. Setup in a few hours. Works as a supplement to Stripe, not a replacement.
- **Shopify Payments:** If you're on Shopify, using their native payments eliminates transaction fees (otherwise Shopify charges 0.5–2% on top of gateway fees). Setup is instant.

For urgent launch: Stripe + PayPal gives you maximum consumer trust with minimum setup time.

### Shipping: Getting This Right on Day One

If shipping isn't configured before launch, you'll lose sales. US customers expect real-time shipping rates at checkout.

**Shopify Shipping** gives you discounted USPS, UPS, and DHL rates with automatic label printing. Setup takes under an hour.

**ShipStation** integrates with almost every platform and carrier. For merchants on WooCommerce or custom builds, this is the fastest multi-carrier setup available.

For under 50 orders/month: direct USPS business account is fine. Above that, a multi-carrier solution like ShipStation or EasyPost gives you better rates and automation.

## The Minimum Your Online Store Needs to Start Selling

Cut everything non-essential. These are the required elements:

**Required for v1:**
- Product catalog with photos, descriptions, prices, and inventory
- Shopping cart and functional checkout
- Online payment (credit/debit + digital wallets like Apple Pay)
- Real-time shipping rate calculation
- Return and refund policy (legally required under FTC guidelines for online retail)
- SSL certificate (HTTPS required by all major payment processors)
- Mobile-optimized design (65%+ of US e-commerce traffic is mobile)

**Can wait for v2:**
- Loyalty program
- Advanced discount rules
- Blog
- Live chat
- Marketplace integrations

Every extra feature in v1 adds 2–5 days to your launch timeline. Be ruthless about scope. Once you're live and generating revenue, optimizing [checkout to reduce cart abandonment](https://systemforgesoftware.com/blog/optimized-checkout-reduce-cart-abandonment) is the highest-ROI next step.

## Common Mistakes When Building an Online Store Fast

**1. Poor product photography.** Low-quality images reduce conversion by up to 40%. You don't need a professional photographer — a modern smartphone with good lighting and a white background works. But don't skip this step.

**2. Copying product descriptions from the manufacturer.** Besides not helping with SEO (duplicate content), generic descriptions don't sell. Write for the customer: what problem does this solve? Who is it for? What makes it different?

**3. Not testing mobile checkout.** More than 65% of US e-commerce purchases happen on mobile. If mobile checkout is clunky, you lose most of your sales. Test every step of the purchase on a phone before going live.

**4. Skipping the return policy.** The FTC and various state consumer protection laws require a clear return policy for online retailers. Without one, you face legal exposure and lose customer trust. It takes 30 minutes to write — do it before you launch.

**5. No sales tax setup.** After the 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair Supreme Court ruling, online sellers may have nexus obligations in states where they have significant sales volume. Shopify Tax and TaxJar automate this. Configure it from day one or you'll have a compliance headache.

**6. Ignoring inventory management.** Once you start selling at volume, stock sync becomes critical. Read our guide on [inventory management to avoid overselling](https://systemforgesoftware.com/blog/inventory-management-ecommerce-avoid-overselling) before you scale.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to build an online store from scratch?

On a SaaS platform (Shopify, BigCommerce), three to seven days with products loaded, payment configured, and shipping set up. A custom store takes 2–4 weeks for a functional MVP. Timeline depends on product count and integration complexity.

### What's the cheapest way to start an online store?

Shopify Basic at $29/month is the most accessible entry point with professional built-in payment processing, shipping, and a solid app ecosystem. WooCommerce on shared hosting can be cheaper, but requires more technical setup. The right answer depends on your specific needs.

### Do I need an LLC or business entity to sell online?

You don't need an LLC to start, but it's strongly recommended before you start processing payments. An LLC provides liability protection, makes it easier to open a business bank account, and is required by many payment processors for higher monthly volumes. Consult a CPA or business attorney in your state.

### Will a quickly built store look unprofessional?

No. Premium themes on platforms like Shopify are professionally designed, mobile-optimized, and fast. Brand customization (logo, colors, fonts, hero banner) takes three to five hours and delivers quality visual results. What matters most to conversion is the shopping experience, not visual effects.

### How do I handle sales tax for an online store?

After the South Dakota v. Wayfair (2018) ruling, you may have economic nexus in states where you exceed sales thresholds (typically $100,000 in revenue or 200 transactions). Shopify Tax, TaxJar, or Avalara automate collection and remittance. Set this up from day one — retroactive compliance is painful.

### Can I sell on Amazon and my own store at the same time?

Yes, and you should. Use tools like Linnworks, Skubana, or Shopify's multi-channel features to centralize inventory. Marketplaces deliver volume (at 8–15% commission). Your own store builds margin and customer relationships. Start both simultaneously.

### What's the minimum I need to accept payments legally?

You need: a verified payment processor account (Stripe, PayPal, or Shopify Payments), SSL certificate on your domain, a privacy policy, terms of service, and a return policy. Stripe and PayPal require ID verification and bank account linking before you can process live payments — start this on day one of setup.
