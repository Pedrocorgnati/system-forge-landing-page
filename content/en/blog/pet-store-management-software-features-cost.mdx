Pet store management software must include grooming appointment scheduling, inventory control for food and medications, animal records, integrated POS, and automated reminder messages via SMS or email. Without these features, you're running on spreadsheets and paper calendars — while the groomer down the street is sending automated return reminders and winning back your customers.

The cost varies: ready-made pet store software runs between $100 and $500/month, while custom systems require $20,000 to $60,000 to develop (with maintenance from $800/month).

## Why Pet Stores Need Industry-Specific Software

A pet store has a mix of needs that no generic retail system handles well:

- **Service scheduling** with time slot, groomer, animal, and owner — it's not just selling products, it's managing grooming bench time
- **Animal records** with service history, vaccines, allergies, and owner preferences
- **Mixed inventory**: pet food (weight/expiration control), medications (lot tracking, prescriptions), accessories
- **Staff commissions** per service completed
- **Owner communication** via SMS, email, or app — return reminders, new product alerts, promotions

A generic ERP or retail point-of-sale system doesn't do grooming appointments or animal medical records. That's why pet stores either use sector-specific software or end up with 3 different tools that don't talk to each other.

## Must-Have Features in Pet Store Management Software

### Grooming Appointment Scheduling

This is the core of any pet shop operation. A solid scheduling module must:
- View by day/week with groomer allocation
- Animal profiles with photo, breed, size, and special notes
- Automatic WhatsApp/SMS/email confirmation to owner
- Automated reminder 24h before appointment
- Late pickup alert when the animal hasn't been collected

### Smart Inventory Control

Pet store inventory has unique characteristics:
- Food: weight, lot, and expiration tracking — automatic alert before expiry
- Medications: prescription tracking, controlled vs. non-controlled separation
- Accessories: standard retail with SKU, barcode, and reorder points
- Supplier purchase history with average lead times

### Animal Records

Each animal should have a profile with:
- Basic info: name, species, breed, date of birth, weight, color
- Service history: dates, groomer, amount, notes
- Purchase history for food and medications
- Allergies and restrictions
- Vaccination records (with optional vet clinic integration)

### Integrated POS and Financial Management

- POS integrated with inventory — automatic stock deduction on sale
- Multiple payment methods: card, cash, recurring billing, Afterpay/Klarna
- Revenue reports by period, service, and product
- Expense tracking and simple cash flow overview

### Owner CRM

- Complete purchase and service history per owner
- Segmentation by visit frequency, animal type, and average spend
- Promotional and reminder messaging via SMS/email
- Loyalty program (points, cashback, complimentary visit)

## When a Custom System Makes More Sense

Ready-made solutions cover 80% of cases. Problems arise when:

- You have multiple locations and need centralized control with unit-specific rules
- You want to integrate scheduling with your own marketplace or pet food delivery app
- You need advanced commission structures (variable rates by service, performance bonuses)
- You want to integrate with a partner veterinary clinic using a specific protocol
- Your store offers pet boarding (lodging) with controls that off-the-shelf systems don't support well

A [custom system](/servicos/custom-systems) for a full-service pet store — scheduling, records, inventory, POS, and SMS/WhatsApp integration — costs between $20,000 and $60,000 to develop, with monthly maintenance between $800 and $1,500. For operations with 3+ staff and revenue above $25,000/month, the investment pays back in 18-24 months through improved customer retention and reduced operational errors.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Pet Store Software

**What's the best software for a small pet store (1-2 staff)?**
For a small operation, 123Pet or Pawfinity handle the basics well. The monthly cost of $39-$99 pays for itself with reduced missed appointments and inventory control that prevents duplicate purchasing. A [free diagnostic](/contato) can point you to the best fit for your operation size.

**Does pet store software work on smartphone?**
Yes. Most leading systems have mobile apps or are fully responsive. For custom systems, the build can include native iOS and Android apps if the use case demands it.

**Can I integrate pet store software with pet food delivery platforms?**
Yes, but it depends on the system. Off-the-shelf solutions typically offer marketplace integrations (Instacart, DoorDash) via paid connectors. Custom systems integrate directly via the delivery platform's API, with no per-order fee.

**How do I manage boarding (overnight stays) alongside grooming?**
Most off-the-shelf systems lack a proper boarding module. If boarding is a significant part of your revenue, that's a clear signal that a custom system is worth the investment — boarding has occupancy, rate, and daily care tracking requirements that generic tools can't accommodate well.

**How long does it take to implement pet store software?**
Off-the-shelf systems are operational in 1-2 weeks (setup + training). Custom systems take 3-5 months to develop, plus 2 weeks for training and data migration.
