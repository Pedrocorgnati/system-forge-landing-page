## What to look for in a Dallas software development partner

**Technical stack alignment** — If you're building a modern web app, your partner should work with React, Next.js, or similar frameworks. If you need mobile, they should have shipped iOS and Android apps to the stores. Ask for live URLs and app store links, not screenshots.

**Industry experience** — A developer who built restaurant apps might struggle with HIPAA compliance. A team that understands healthcare, logistics, or finance will ask better questions upfront and avoid costly rework.

**Communication standards** — DFW business culture moves fast. Your development partner should match that cadence: daily or weekly standups, transparent project tracking, and direct access to the developers building your software — not just a project manager relaying messages.

**Contract structure and IP ownership** — You should own 100% of the code, designs, and documentation from day one. Milestone-based payments tied to accepted deliverables protect both sides. Avoid contracts that demand more than 30–40% upfront.
