## Software development in the Research Triangle — what NC companies are building

The Research Triangle Park is the largest research park in the US — 7,000+ acres and 300+ companies. That scale creates software demand unlike anywhere else in the Southeast.

**Life sciences and biotech software** — RTP hosts GlaxoSmithKline, Bayer, Novo Nordisk, and hundreds of smaller biotech firms. North Carolina ranks in the top three for life sciences employment growth. Software needs include laboratory information management systems (LIMS), clinical trial data platforms, regulatory submission tools, and supply chain tracking for pharmaceutical manufacturing.

**Healthcare technology** — UNC Health, Duke Health, and surrounding practices need patient portals, telehealth platforms, and operational systems — all HIPAA-compliant. The clinical research overlap between healthcare and pharma creates unique requirements for de-identified data handling and protocol management.

**Enterprise software and SaaS** — SAS Institute and Red Hat (now IBM) have anchored the Triangle's enterprise software culture for decades. That heritage means Triangle buyers expect architectural rigor: proper API design, scalable infrastructure, and clean codebases. SaaS products built here often serve professional and B2B markets rather than consumer apps.

**Edtech platforms** — Duke, UNC Chapel Hill, and NC State generate a constant pipeline of educational technology startups. These platforms need Learning Management System integrations, assessment tools, and analytics dashboards — often with FERPA compliance requirements.

**Startup web apps and B2B SaaS** — HQ Raleigh and the Council for Entrepreneurial Development (CED) support a growing startup ecosystem. Notable Triangle companies include Pendo, Bandwidth, and Dude Solutions. The B2B focus means software here prioritizes functionality, integrations, and ROI over flashy design.

## Research Triangle's specific software requirements

**HIPAA compliance for health-adjacent applications** — Any software touching PHI needs Business Associate Agreements, encryption, access controls, and audit logging. Life sciences companies handling patient data in clinical trials face HIPAA implications even when they're not traditional healthcare providers.

**Scientific data handling** — Biotech software must handle structured and unstructured experimental data, often with strict chain-of-custody requirements. Laboratory information systems need sample tracking, instrument integration, and regulatory-compliant record keeping.

**Open source heritage** — Red Hat's presence in RTP created a local culture that values open source, transparent architecture, and community contribution. Triangle technical buyers often prefer technology stacks with strong open-source ecosystems: PostgreSQL, Python, Node.js, React.

**Enterprise integration requirements** — Large RTP employers like IBM (10,000+ employees in RTP), Cisco, and SAS run on complex enterprise stacks. Any software serving these ecosystems needs robust API architecture, SSO integration, and the ability to push and pull data from SAP, Salesforce, or custom internal systems.
